Emily Conover

I’m a physics reporter at Science News, with a PhD in physics and an enduring curiosity about our world, our universe, and the way things work.

I’ve covered physics for Science News since 2016. I got my PhD from the University of Chicago in 2014, where I studied the idiosyncrasies of subatomic particles called neutrinos. I’m a past president of the D.C. Science Writers Association.

I previously wrote for the American Physical Society, Science Magazine and, through a AAAS Mass Media Fellowship, the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel.
