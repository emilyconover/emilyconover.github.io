About

I cover physics and related fields for Science News, where I write about everything from particle physics and cosmology to quantum computing and materials science.

Before becoming a journalist, I earned a PhD in physics from the University of Chicago, where I studied neutrinos. I am currently president of the D.C. Science Writers Association.
